# Carlos — Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for **Carlos**, a local-only iOS app for car costs and mileage.

**Read the policy:** [alebree.github.io/carlos-privacy](https://alebree.github.io/carlos-privacy/)

Carlos stores vehicle, expense and odometer data only on your device. There is no account, no Carlos cloud, and no advertising or analytics tracking in the current app.

Contact: [carlos-car-app@proton.me](mailto:carlos-car-app@proton.me)
